# Source and copyright policy

Use this policy before collecting prescription content.

## Preferred sources

1. User-provided licensed material with permission to transform and store metadata.
2. Public-domain classical texts where local copyright status is clear.
3. Official clinical guidelines, pharmacopeia entries, hospital education materials, or regulator pages.
4. Bibliographic metadata from copyrighted books, with short summaries written in new wording.

## Do not ingest

- Full copyrighted book chapters or large copied formula collections.
- Paywalled database content without permission.
- Forum posts or commercial ads that lack provenance, clinician attribution, or safety context.
- Any content that promises guaranteed cures, especially for cancer, infertility, chronic viral disease, or emergency conditions.

## Minimum provenance fields

- Title, author/editor or organization.
- Edition, publication date, or page version date.
- URL, page number, or section name.
- Access/extraction date.
- License or permission status.
- Human reviewer or review queue status.

## AI API quota verification

- Treat free tiers and trial credits as volatile.
- Verify only against official provider domains or authenticated dashboards.
- Store `lastVerified` and a short note such as `official-doc-required` when exact limits must be checked by a human.
