# Loose wellness floral tea / 散装养生花茶 guide

Use this reference when collecting or presenting 清火茶、祛湿茶、润喉茶、瘦身茶、美白茶 or premium loose wellness floral tea Q&A.

## Safe framing

- Treat these products as food/beverage wellness references unless a licensed medicine source says otherwise.
- Do not claim disease treatment, guaranteed weight loss, whitening, detox cures, or drug replacement.
- Keep batch, origin, material, allergen, storage, and brewing metadata separate from medical claims.
- Mark user-provided WeChat links as `needs_manual_verification` unless the page content is accessible and permission is clear.

## Catalog categories

1. **清火茶**: chrysanthemum, honeysuckle, dandelion, mint, lophatherum, green tea; caution for cold digestion and diarrhea.
2. **祛湿茶**: coix seed, adzuki bean, poria, aged tangerine peel, lotus leaf, patchouli; caution for pregnancy, kidney disease, unexplained edema, and diuretics.
3. **润喉茶**: monk fruit, ophiopogon, platycodon, licorice, chrysanthemum, boat-fruited sterculia; caution with long-term use and licorice interactions.
4. **瘦身茶**: lotus leaf, hawthorn, cassia seed, aged tangerine peel, oolong, rose; do not promise rapid weight loss or include laxative abuse.
5. **美白茶**: rose, roselle, goji berry, mulberry, jasmine, osmanthus; describe as complexion/hydration support, not medical whitening.
6. **精品散装养生花茶**: organize by loose-weight sale, custom blend, gift set, storage, origin, and compliance labels.
