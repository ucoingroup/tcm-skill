# Bilingual translation rules

- Keep the Chinese formula and herb names as the canonical labels.
- Add English names for discoverability, but do not replace Chinese materia medica terminology.
- Translate TCM actions literally and consistently: 宣肺 `diffuse Lung qi`, 化痰 `transform phlegm`, 活血 `move blood`, 祛湿 `dispell dampness`, 温阳 `warm yang`.
- Keep pattern differentiation explicit. Do not translate a biomedical disease into a single formula recommendation.
- Preserve processing terms: 炙 `honey-fried or prepared as specified`, 炒 `dry-fried`, 煅 `calcined`, 酒洗 `wine-washed`.
- Mark uncertain names as `needs terminology review` instead of inventing a Latin or pharmaceutical equivalent.
- Translate cautions in plain language for safety, including pregnancy, children, older adults, liver/kidney disease, and herb-drug interactions.
