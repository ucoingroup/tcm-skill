# Safety review checklist

Always include these checks before presenting a TCM prescription:

1. **Professional guidance**: state that formulas are for reference/research and require a qualified TCM clinician.
2. **Pattern differentiation**: do not map a biomedical disease directly to one formula without syndrome differentiation.
3. **Toxic/restricted herbs**: flag ingredients such as 附子, 乌头, 马钱子, 朱砂, 雄黄, 细辛, 半夏, 南星 and any jurisdiction-restricted materia medica.
4. **Pregnancy/lactation**: flag blood-moving, purgative, toxic, or strong downward-directing herbs.
5. **Children/elderly**: flag dose uncertainty and higher vulnerability.
6. **Comorbidities**: flag liver/kidney disease, anticoagulants, antiplatelets, chemotherapy, immunosuppression, and chronic medication use.
7. **Red flags**: chest pain, severe dyspnea, stroke signs, GI bleeding, high fever, severe abdominal pain, altered consciousness, cancer alarm symptoms, or abnormal uterine bleeding require urgent medical care.
8. **Copyright**: summarize and structure; do not copy protected source text beyond brief fair-use excerpts.
